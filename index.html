About the Event

Sydney Hydrology Day is an annual one-day forum that brings together researchers and professionals across Sydney to share advances in hydrology and water science more generally.
This years theme is: Managing water as the landscape connector under future climate conditions
The program blends a panel discussion and themed presentation sessions to highlight current research, practical applications, and emerging methods in Hydrology and the Science of Water across the Universities and research institutions in the Sydney Basin. With a strong focus on postgraduate and early‑career work, the day fosters collaboration between universities and industry and strengthens the hydrology community within the Sydney region.

We invite contributions from researchers across Engineering and Environmental Science, with areas of interest such as water quality, water quantity, ecohydrology, water ecology and water management


The 2027 event will take place on 9 February 2027.

Program

We are pleased to announce that Professor Kristie Fryirs has kindly agreed to provide the opening keynote for the conference. Prof Fryirs work focuses on fluvial geomorphology and river management. She researches the structure and function of rivers, how they adjust and evolve, how they have been impacted by anthropogenic disturbance and how to best use geomorphology in river conservation, recovery and rehabilitation. She is probably best know for her work on the River Styles Framework, a geomorphic approach to the analysis of rivers, supporting management practice.
